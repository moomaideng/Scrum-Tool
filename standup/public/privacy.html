Privacy policy

Eventory Standup is a small team tool for recording daily Scrum updates.

Information we collect

When you sign in, we receive your Google account identifier, name, email address, and profile picture. We also store the Done, To do, and Problem answers you submit.

How information is used

We use this information to authenticate you, display team standups, maintain sprint history, synchronize records to the team's Google Sheet, and email a reminder when an active member has not submitted by the daily deadline.

Who can see information

Signed-in users can see registered members and shared standup entries. The administrator can manage sprints and reminder participation. Data is also written to a private Google Sheet shared by the team.

Storage and sharing

Application data is stored on the team's server. We use Google Identity Services for login, Google Sheets for synchronization, and Gmail or another configured SMTP service for reminders. We do not sell information or use it for advertising.

Retention and requests

Standup history is retained for the team's project records. Contact the Eventory team administrator if you want your account or entries corrected or removed.
